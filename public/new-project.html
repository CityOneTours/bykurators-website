Studio Juli Atelier
Design Philosophy

Juli Atelier creates interiors with narrative — spaces that hold the character of the people who inhabit them and the identity of the brands that occupy them. Founded by Julia Sakhno, the studio brings together interior design, environmental psychology, and emotional intelligence to produce work that operates on multiple registers: beautiful to inhabit, purposeful to experience, and enduring in value. Her approach is deeply personal without ever being precious — each project is crafted to feel inevitable.

Project Types

The studio works across luxury residential, hospitality, and branded commercial environments, with a particular strength in high-specification private residences and bespoke retail spaces. Projects range from complete design journeys — concept through final styling — to carefully curated interventions, and may include art curation, antiques sourcing, textile design, and custom decorative detailing.
