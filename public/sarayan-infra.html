Design Philosophy

Sarayan Infra LLP designs spaces that perform — not just impress. Built on the 25+ year legacy of Sarayan Architects, the practice is led by Ar. Pankaj Lad and Ar. Namrata Lad, who bring together research, human behaviour, systems thinking, design intelligence, and execution expertise to create environments that strengthen culture, elevate brand value, and deliver measurable business impact.

Within this approach, sustainability is treated not as an add-on, but as a design responsibility — through resource-conscious planning, material sensitivity, long-term usability, and environments that support both people and the planet. Every project is approached as one connected system — strategy, design, detailing, coordination, procurement, execution, and outcome — with every stage working seamlessly together from concept to handover. The result is spaces that are not merely built, but thoughtfully engineered to work harder for the people and businesses inside them.

Project Types

The studio works across corporate, commercial, healthcare, hospitality, and residential environments, with particular strength in large-scale workplace delivery. With 250+ workspaces transformed and over 2 million sq. ft. delivered across India, Sarayan Infra LLP brings end-to-end capability across strategy, architecture, interiors, sustainable design, human-centric spaces, design-build, and integrated execution.
