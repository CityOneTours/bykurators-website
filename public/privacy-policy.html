01Who we are

By Kurators is the trading name of ByKurators Project Consulting – FZCO, a design curation and project management consultancy based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates (“By Kurators”, “we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

We handle personal data in accordance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and other applicable UAE laws.

02What we collect

We collect only what we need to understand your project and respond to you.

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Project details — property type, location or community, project stage, indicative budget, preferred timeline, and any design preferences or files you choose to share.
  • Enquiry source — when you contact us through an advertisement, such as an Instagram or Facebook lead form, we receive the details you submit together with the name of the advertisement and form.
  • Communications — records of emails, calls, WhatsApp messages and meetings with you.
  • Technical information — basic data such as browser type, device and pages visited, collected when you use our website.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, sensitive personal data such as health, religious or financial account information.

03How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To assess your brief and prepare a shortlist of design studios suited to your home, taste and budget.
  • To introduce you to, and coordinate with, the design studios and contractors involved in your project.
  • To manage and deliver projects, including scheduling, documentation and invoicing.
  • To send occasional updates about our work, only where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To improve our website, services and advertising.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

04Legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit an enquiry or form; where it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into, or to perform, a contract with you; and where it is necessary to comply with our legal obligations in the UAE. You may withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before you withdrew it.

05Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only where needed, and only with:

  • Design partner studios on our roster, in the UAE and abroad, so that they can consider and prepare proposals for your project. We share only the details relevant to your brief.
  • Contractors and suppliers engaged on your project, where you have asked us to manage delivery.
  • Service providers who support our business, such as customer relationship management, email, cloud storage and advertising platforms (for example, Zoho and Meta). They may process data only on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, such as auditors, legal advisers, or government bodies where required by law.

06International transfers

Many of the studios we work with are based outside the UAE, including in India, and some of our service providers store data on servers outside the UAE. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we do so as permitted under UAE law and take reasonable steps — including contractual safeguards — to ensure it remains protected.

07How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months from our last contact with you if a project does not go ahead, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. For clients, we keep project records for as long as needed to deliver the project and afterwards for the period required by UAE commercial, accounting and tax law. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

08Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls, secure business systems and limiting access to people who need it for their work. No method of transmission or storage is entirely secure, but we take the protection of your information seriously and review our measures regularly.

09Your rights

Subject to UAE law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to delete your data, or to restrict how we use it.
  • Object to processing, including for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Ask us to transfer your data to another organisation where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@bykurators.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time by replying “stop” or using the link in the message. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise a complaint with the UAE Data Office.

10Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep it working properly, understand how it is used, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising, for example through the Meta Pixel. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date it was last updated. Our services are intended for adults and are not directed at anyone under 18.

12Contact us

For any question about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us.
